Privacy Policy

Order Task Tracker is a private tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own Google account.

  • What it accesses: read-only access to the owner's Gmail, to find order and shipping emails, and access to the owner's Google Tasks, to create and update order tasks.
  • What it keeps: order details extracted from those emails (retailer, items, order number, tracking number, dates), stored in the owner's private database.
  • What it never does: send, delete, or modify email; share, sell, or disclose any data to third parties; access any other person's account.
  • Processing: email text is processed by Anthropic's Claude to extract order details.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
